What we do

We focus on two core pillars that directly affect business growth: bespoke web engineering and high-intent Meta advertising.

01 / Core Service

Website Development

We design and build bespoke, responsive websites engineered for speed, search visibility, and conversion. Every project is tailored specifically to your audience and operational needs:

  • E-Commerce Platforms: Fast-loading online stores integrated with local payment gateways (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, SSLCommerz) and international gateways (Stripe, PayPal).
  • School & College Portals: Centralized digital administration for admissions, computerized fee payments, and staff records.
  • Corporate & Private Company Sites: Institutional platforms built for credibility, compliance, and enterprise lead capture.
  • Portfolios: Distinctive showcases for creators, founders, and specialized service firms.

02 / Growth Service

Meta Ads Support

Paid social advertising designed for verifiable business outcomes. We manage campaigns across Facebook and Instagram to drive qualified customer acquisition, brand reach, and high-intent leads:

  • Server-Side Tracking (CAPI): Full Conversions API integration to ensure reliable data measurement independent of browser ad-blockers.
  • Lead Generation Funnels: Direct-to-WhatsApp chats, customized lead forms, and CRM routing for B2B and B2C clients.
  • Multi-Stage Retargeting: Re-engaging warm visitors, video viewers, and cart abandoners with promotional incentives.
  • Weekly Reporting: Transparent performance reviews focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) and cost per acquired lead.
